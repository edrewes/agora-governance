# Agora

Agora is an evidence-grounded research and design initiative for understanding how people govern together—and how institutions, technology, and AI can support legitimate, capable, and flourishing societies.

We study historical and contemporary governance forms across cultures and scales; human–AI governance; institutional design; delegated authority; and technology-enabled coordination. We aim to publish research papers, design theories, practical playbooks, and reusable digital tools. We do not assume one governance model is universal, and we do not treat AI efficiency as an adequate measure of human progress.

## Operating loop

1. Frame a falsifiable or decision-relevant question; specify population, institutional context, and time horizon.
2. Search primary sources and suitable scholarship; record search date, provenance, access limits, and counter-evidence.
3. Compare mechanisms and outcomes across contexts without collapsing unlike institutions into a single score.
4. Separate description, causal evidence, interpretation, proposal, and uncertainty.
5. Review for cultural coverage, rights, affected-party voice, distributional effects, and plausible failure modes.
6. Publish only within the evidence and review gates in the protocol; retain corrections and version history.

## Status

This is a research scaffold, not a validated expert system. Skills define repeatable workflows; they do not certify expertise, guarantee current coverage, or authorize policy decisions. The initial GitHub repository is the source of truth. Any future agent is a research assistant operating under these methods, not an autonomous publisher or decision-maker.
